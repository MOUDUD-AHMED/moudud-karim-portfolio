FAQ

Need something more specific? Send your targeting criteria and project requirements.

What information can you provide?

Depending on the project, fields can include company name, website, contact name, job title, LinkedIn profile, business email, phone, location, and other requested business information where available.

Can you work with custom targeting criteria?

Yes. Research can be based on industry, location, company type, decision-maker title, and other project-specific criteria.

How do you verify email addresses?

I use dedicated email verification tools such as NeverBounce, ZeroBounce, MillionVerifier, Verifalia, and MailTester depending on the project.

Can you enrich my existing database?

Yes. I can research missing company or contact information and add relevant fields to an existing list.

What format will I receive?

Lead data can be organized in a clean spreadsheet structure based on the fields required for your project.

Can you handle large lead lists?

Yes. My featured projects include lists of 1,000, 1,700, and 2,000 prospects.

Can I review a sample first?

Yes. Send your targeting criteria and I can prepare a small sample so you can review the structure and data quality before discussing a larger project.
